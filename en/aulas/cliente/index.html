Lesson 6 · 15 min

New client: the checklist that stops when something fails

You signed a new client. Now you need to update the contact record, create a folder, set up the schedule, prepare the first invoice, and write a welcome message. It's the same five steps every time, and forgetting one can be costly. The agent goes through the checklist in order, doesn't create anything twice, and stops at the step that fails.

Start the lesson

In this lesson, you’ll

  1. 1Turn your routine into a checklist
  2. 2Run it step by step, marking each one done
  3. 3Stop at the step that fails and say why
  4. 4Resume without redoing what’s already done

Goal: write the new client checklist for the agent to follow in order, stopping at a failed step without creating duplicates.

01 · The five steps, in order

Click each step. The last one is always a draft.

If a step fails, it stops and tells you which one and why. Next time, it continues from there.

03 · The checklist sheet

Replace the steps with yours. Keep the order and rules.

Sheet · new client agent
Role: You are my new client agent.
About me: [what the business does]; folder template: [name]; schedule template: [name]; welcome email template: [text or name]; existing clients and folders: [paste the list].
When: when I say "new client: [name]" and paste the proposal or details.
Steps: 1) customer record: status "closed" and start date; 2) folder with the client's name, based on the template; 3) schedule based on the template, with dates starting from the start date; 4) first invoice as a draft, with the amount of the proposal's first installment; 5) welcome email as a draft, with links to the folder and schedule.
Rules: do the steps in order and mark each as done; if a client, folder, or invoice with that name already exists, don't create another one: stop and tell me which step and what you found; if a step fails, stop there; invoice and email as drafts only, I’ll issue and send them; never ask for a password; if any information is missing, stop and ask.
Delivery: the list of the 5 steps marked done / stopped and the reason, the links or names created, the invoice amount, and "email in draft".
First task: repeat the checklist in your own words and wait for me to say "new client".
In chat · the request
new client: Padaria Trigo Bom

Existing clients and folders: Marcenaria Lopes, Clínica Vida.
Proposal details: contact Marta Reis (marta@exemplo.com), custom industrial kitchen, start date 10/11, total amount 18.000,00 in 3 installments, first due at signing.
In chat, you don't create the folder or customer record: give me the ready-to-use text for each step (folder name, schedule in a table, invoice draft, email draft), and I’ll click through and mark each one done.
Terminal (optional) · real folders on your computer
cd meus-agentes/clientes
# the sheet is in AGENTS.md; the template is in clientes/modelo/
codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -m gpt-6-luna -s workspace-write "new client: Padaria Trigo Bom — follow AGENTS.md"
Simulation: illustrative example

In chat, the agent prepares the text for each step and you do the clicks: it's the checklist with the boring parts (writing, calculating dates, putting the email together) taken care of. In the terminal, with -s workspace-write, Codex can copy the template folder inside the folder you're in; it can't copy outside it. You still handle the client record in an online system and the invoice.

  1. All five steps appear in order, each marked done or stopped.
  2. The invoice is 6.000,00 (the first of 3 installments of 18.000,00) and is a draft.
  3. If you send the same request again in the same conversation, the second round stops right away at the first step because the client already exists.
  • Checklist with no set order: the email goes out before the folder exists, so the link is broken.
  • "If it fails, try another way": the agent improvises and creates the folder "Padaria Trigo Bom (2)". If something fails, stop.
  • Set the invoice to "issue": money and a client mean at least medium risk; always use a draft.

04 · Practice: your checklist

Write down the routine you already follow today.

E1 · The routine on paper

10 min

List what you do today when you sign a client, in order. Trim it to the 5 steps you repeat most often.

Done when: the list has 4 to 6 numbered steps, and each one says which tool it's done in.

E2 · The sheet

15 min

Adapt the sheet with your steps and templates, and put it in a Project (or a folder in the terminal).

Done when: the agent repeats the checklist in its own words, in the right order, and waits for "new client".

E3 · Practice with a fictional client

10 min

Run it with Padaria Trigo Bom. Then send the same request again in the same conversation (in chat, "already exists" depends on the list you pasted and what's in the conversation).

Done when: the first round delivers the 5 steps and the 6.000,00 invoice as a draft; the second stops and lets you know the client already exists.

06 · The lesson in three sentences

Fixed orderThe same steps, always in the same order, each marked done.
Failed, stopAlready exists or something went wrong: the agent stops, says which step it reached and what it saw, and doesn't improvise.
Resume without redoing workOn the next run, pick up where you left off. No duplicates.
